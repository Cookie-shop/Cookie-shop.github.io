FAQ

Simple answers before checkout.

How do orders work?

Add products to the cart, copy the order summary, and paste it into a Discord ticket. Staff confirms stock, delivery details, and payment.

Does the cart save?

Yes. Your cart and currency choice are saved in this browser so you can return without rebuilding the order.

What if an image does not load?

The product card still works, and the cart can still add the product. Replace the asset file anytime without changing the product data.

Can I pay with balance?

Yes, when the connected backend is online and your account has enough balance. Otherwise use a Discord ticket.
