FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything important before you place an order.

?How do orders work?

Add products to the cart, click Pay With PayPal, and pay with the shown Order ID. After confirmation, you get a delivery code for Discord.

$What payment methods do you accept?

PayPal, Swedbank, SEB, and Luminor are shown as payment options. Use the exact amount and include the generated Order ID in the payment note.

THow long does delivery take?

Delivery depends on staff confirmation. The PayPal flow auto-checks every 20 seconds, and Discord support can help if anything takes longer.

CDoes the cart save?

Yes. Your cart and currency choice are saved in this browser so you can return without rebuilding the order.

IWhat if an image does not load?

The product card still works, and the cart can still add the product. Replace the asset file anytime without changing the product data.

DWhere does the code go?

After payment is confirmed, paste the delivery code into your Discord ticket so staff can finish the order.
