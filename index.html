FAQ

Simple answers before checkout.

How do orders work?

Add products to the cart, click Pay With PayPal, and pay with the shown Order ID. After confirmation, you get a delivery code for Discord.

Does the cart save?

Yes. Your cart and currency choice are saved in this browser so you can return without rebuilding the order.

What if an image does not load?

The product card still works, and the cart can still add the product. Replace the asset file anytime without changing the product data.

Where does the code go?

After payment is confirmed, paste the delivery code into your Discord ticket so staff can finish the order.
